On **5–6 March 2025**, IEEE-CIET and the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Chitkara University ran **Hack With Her 4.0**, a 30-hour overnight hackathon for women in tech. As WIE Vice Chairperson, I was part of the team that made it happen.

### The event you see is 10% of the work

Participants see two days. Organisers see weeks of venue bookings, sponsor calls, judging criteria, food schedules and a plan for what happens at 3 a.m. when the Wi-Fi drops. Most of the work is invisible, and the event goes well only if that invisible work was done.

### Mentors change the quality of everything

Mentors from **Edifecs** worked with teams through the night. Teams that took feedback early ended up with sharper, more realistic solutions. Getting the right mentors in the room was one of the best decisions we made.

### What I'd tell the next organiser

- Write a run-sheet down to 30-minute blocks, and give every block an owner.
- Plan for the night shift. Energy drops after midnight, so schedule a check-in or a fun round then.
- Thank people by name. Faculty, volunteers and the core team carried this event.

> **Takeaway:** leadership at an event like this is mostly logistics, delegation and staying calm when something breaks.
